**Pitch:** One room exists twice, a colourful life and a red life laid over each other, and you walk through portal arches between them to get around what blocks you in the other.

**Genre:** 3D puzzle

**Controls:** W A S D or the Up and Down arrows to walk, the mouse (click the game) or the Left and Right arrows to look around, E or Space to pick up a crate or put it down ahead, Z to undo, R to restart, P to pause, M to mute, Esc to free the mouse. Walking through an arch changes life. *(guessed)*

**Question:** Is crossing between two overlaid worlds to solve a room satisfying for ten minutes? *(guessed)*

**Hooks:** Each arch is a window: through it you see the other life at the same spot, live. The same tile is a wall or water in one life and open floor in the other, and what you carry comes with you.

**Intent:** soft or no failure *(provisional)*

## First minute
You stand in a sunny walled garden with the exit star in sight behind a wall you cannot pass. Ahead is a glowing arch, and through it the same floor shows in red. You walk through, the world turns red, the wall is gone on the far side of the room, you cross over, walk through a second arch, and you are back in colour beside the exit. The star lights. Room 2 opens with a switch, a door, and a crate that exists only in the red life.

## Challenge
*(provisional)* Room 3, around minute three, brings the first real puzzle. A switch in the colourful life needs a crate that exists only in the red one, and the nearest arch puts it on the wrong bank of a river. No death and nothing lost: undo takes back any move and restart resets the room. Rooms 4 and 5 add water a crate can bridge and switches that open doors in the other life.

## Core loop
- Read the room in both lives through the arches.
- Cross, move a crate or press a switch, and see what changed in the other life.
- The exit lights when the way is open; walk to it for the next room.
- A wrong idea costs one undo.

## Goal & fail state
- Reach the exit of each of five short rooms; after the fifth, an ending screen. *(provisional)*
- No fail state: undo and restart, and a quiet prompt if a crate a switch needed is gone. *(provisional)*
